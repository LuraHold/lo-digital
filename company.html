Company

The American company behind the stores.

Purpose

The company exists to operate digital apparel retail. It owns and runs branded online stores, sells clothing to consumers, collects payment for those sales, and pays the suppliers, warehouses, and service providers that make the stores work.

The Articles of Organization allow any lawful activity under Wyoming law. In practice, the business is electronic shopping of apparel (NAICS 454110). That is the activity described to banks, processors, and counterparties.

The LLC is newly formed. The retail group it serves is not. The United States entity was organized so the group could hold a clear American company, a federal EIN, and a single principal office for banking, tax, and vendor contracts.

Governance

LO Digital LLC is member-managed. The sole member is Lucas Oliveira Silva, who holds 100% of the membership interest and may bind the company in the ordinary course of business.

The Operating Agreement authorizes the member to open bank accounts, keep records, and file tax returns. Profits and losses are allocated to the member in proportion to ownership.

The company is a legal entity separate from its member. Assets are held in the name of LO Digital LLC.
