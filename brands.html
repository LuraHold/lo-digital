Brands

More than 25 clothing stores, under one LLC.

The group sells apparel online. Three house brands are named here because they are the stores we can identify in full. The other storefronts follow the same retail model in their own markets.

PunkStore

PunkStore

A general apparel store with a street-led mix. The catalog is built around graphic shirts, layers, trousers, and accessories that read as youth and urban clothing. Customers are individual shoppers buying finished garments for personal use. PunkStore is an owned online store of LO Digital LLC, not a franchise and not a marketplace for other sellers.

FreelexStore

FreelexStore

Contemporary everyday clothing. FreelexStore sells wearable basics and seasonal pieces: tops, bottoms, knit, and light outerwear. The customer is a general apparel shopper looking for simple, current clothes at consumer prices. Sales are direct to consumer through the brand’s own checkout.

CardoStore

CardoStore

Classic wardrobe clothing. CardoStore focuses on elevated casual staples: shirts, trousers, outer layers, and related accessories meant to be worn often. The customer is an adult shopper buying durable, straightforward pieces. Orders are placed online and shipped as physical goods.

The rest of the group

Beyond the three named brands, LO Digital LLC operates additional online clothing stores. Together the portfolio exceeds 25 storefronts. Those stores sell apparel in the same way: a branded catalog, consumer checkout, and shipment of physical clothing.

Markets include North America, Latin America, Europe, and further regions reached by online retail. A store may be localized in language and catalog. Ownership and treasury sit with LO Digital LLC.

This corporate site does not take consumer orders. Each store keeps its own shop, cart, and customer-service channel. Group policies for privacy, cookies, and returns are published here so counterparties can see the standard that applies across the portfolio.
