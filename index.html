What this company does

We run apparel stores. We sell clothes to people.

LO Digital LLC is the United States company for a retail group that already operates more than 25 online clothing stores. The LLC was organized on September 2, 2026, to hold brand operations, receive customer payments, pay vendors, and manage the group from a single American entity.

Customers are individual shoppers. They buy shirts, trousers, outerwear, and related accessories on our branded websites. Orders are paid by card and other standard checkout methods, then shipped as physical goods.

This site is the corporate presence of the LLC. Purchases are completed on each store’s own checkout, under terms consistent with the group policies published here.

From order to door

How a garment leaves the group.

This is ordinary retail. A person buys clothing. The company ships a physical item. Nothing else is sold.

  1. 01

    The customer chooses clothing on a branded store and pays at checkout by card or another standard method.

  2. 02

    The order is recorded against LO Digital LLC. Settlement is paid to the company, not to a third-party wallet.

  3. 03

    The garment is packed and handed to a carrier. It is a physical good, shipped to the address the customer gave.

  4. 04

    Returns follow the group policy: unused items within 30 days, refunded to the original payment method.

Markets

Stores adapted by country, owned in one place.

The group sells online across regions. Language and catalog may change. Ownership, EIN, and treasury do not.

North America

English-language storefronts selling apparel to consumers in the United States and neighboring markets, settled to the Wyoming company.

Latin America

Localized clothing shops for shoppers in the region. Same model: catalog, checkout, shipment of garments.

Europe and further

Additional online stores serving European and other customers. Each shop is retail. The parent is LO Digital LLC.

How money moves

A retail company, not a financial company.

What we sell

Physical apparel and accessories: clothing for men, women, and unisex wear, plus related items such as bags and small goods.

Who pays us

End customers paying at checkout on our store websites. We do not take deposits, hold client funds, or sell financial products.

How we get paid

Card and other standard e-commerce payment methods through licensed processors. Settlement is paid to LO Digital LLC.

How we deliver

Orders are packed and shipped as physical goods to the address the customer provides. Each store publishes shipping and return rules.
