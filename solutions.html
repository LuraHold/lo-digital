Solutions

Apparel retail, run as a single operating company.

LO Digital LLC does not sell consulting packages to the public. The “solutions” of this company are the functions required to operate more than 25 clothing stores: catalog, checkout, fulfillment, and brand management.

Brand operations

Each store has a name, an assortment, and a customer. The company sets identity, product mix, and the commercial rules that apply across PunkStore, FreelexStore, CardoStore, and the rest of the group.

Digital commerce

We operate online storefronts. Customers browse clothing, place orders, and pay at checkout. The company maintains the sites, catalogs, and order flow behind those stores.

Merchandising

Assortment is clothing in the general sense: tops, bottoms, outerwear, and accessories. We select, price, and refresh product so each store can sell a coherent wardrobe.

Market operations

Stores serve shoppers in multiple countries. Language, catalog, and shipping rules are adapted by market while ownership stays with LO Digital LLC.

Fulfillment

Physical goods are packed and shipped to the customer. The company coordinates warehouses, carriers, and returns so an order becomes a delivered garment.

Treasury for retail

Customer payments settle to the company. The company pays vendors, platforms, and logistics partners. This is ordinary retail cash management, not money transmission for third parties.

Who we serve

Customers. Individual consumers who buy clothing for themselves or as gifts. They are not other banks, not wholesale marketplaces as the primary model, and not clients depositing funds.

What they buy. Apparel and accessories. Typical order values are consumer retail amounts paid at checkout.

How they benefit. They receive the garments they ordered, with shipping and a published return window.

What this company is not

LO Digital LLC is not a bank, payment institution, money services business, investment adviser, or cryptocurrency platform. It does not hold customer balances, issue stored value, or process payments for unrelated merchants. Revenue is from the sale of clothing.
