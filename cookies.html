Cookie policy

Effective September 9, 2026 · LO Digital LLC

This corporate website is a static information site. It does not set advertising cookies and does not require an account.

1. What we use today

  • No first-party tracking cookies on this corporate site.
  • Essential local behavior such as the contact form running in your browser. That does not store a cookie.
  • Fonts loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of the request. See the privacy policy.

2. Store sites

PunkStore, FreelexStore, CardoStore, and the other shops may use cookies that are required for the cart, login, and fraud checks, and may use analytics cookies. Those shops will show their own notice at checkout or in the store footer.

3. How to control cookies

You can block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies on a store site may prevent checkout from completing.
